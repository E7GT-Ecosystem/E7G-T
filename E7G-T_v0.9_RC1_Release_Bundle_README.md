---

## 2. Short Public Definition

> E7G-T is a geometry-first modelling language and practical calculus for making projection, loss, reconstruction, bridge mode, and admissible use visible.

Expanded:

> E7G-T helps people inspect representations before relying on them. It asks what a chart, AI answer, proof text, translation, model, legal summary, or dashboard preserves, what it loses, what source it points to, what claim strength it can support, and what the next responsible move should be.

---

## 3. What E7G-T Is Not

E7G-T is not established mathematics.

E7G-T is not empirical physics.

E7G-T is not a proof system.

E7G-T is not a Theory of Everything.

E7G-T does not replace legal, medical, financial, engineering, translation, statistical, scientific, or formal-methods expertise.

Its proper role is to frame, inspect, document, teach, bridge, lint, and discipline reasoning around representations.

## 7. Recommended First Test

Use **PG-002 Worksheet + PILOT-001** on five real AI answers:

1. current factual answer;
2. legal/admin answer;
3. translation QA answer;
4. business/chart interpretation;
5. proof sketch or technical answer.

Compare ordinary review with E7G-T worksheet review.

Ask:

> Did E7G-T catch a risk or clarify a next move that ordinary review missed?

---

## 8. Recommended Second Test

Use **APP-002 + PILOT-002** on one or two real or anonymised translations.

Best first translation types:

- civil status certificate;
- company register extract;
- official letter;
- apostilled document;
- OCR-heavy scan.

Ask:

> Did E7G-T improve identity/date/number/documentary artefact/formatting/severity checks?

## 9. Reviewer Questions

Ask a reviewer:

1. Does E7G-T read as a modelling language rather than a grandiose theory?
2. Is the kernel clear enough?
3. Are D0–D7 and TR0–TR6 understandable?
4. Are bridge modes useful or too heavy?
5. Does the AI worksheet improve source-return discipline?
6. Does the translation protocol improve QA?
7. Does the dashboard worksheet prevent causal overread?
8. Does Proof-of-Path clarify proof status versus explanation status?
9. Which artifact is strongest?
10. Which artifact should be simplified?
11. What should be tested first?
12. What wording risks overclaiming?

---

## 10. v1.0 Gate

Do not move to v1.0 until:

- terms are stable;
- notation is stable;
- bridge modes are consistently enforced;
- at least three Application Atlas entries are tested;
- at least two worksheets are tested on real examples;
- at least one Proof-of-Path worked example is checked in a real proof assistant or clearly labelled illustrative;
- source-backed references are cleaned;
- public language is simplified;
- overclaim risks are reduced.
