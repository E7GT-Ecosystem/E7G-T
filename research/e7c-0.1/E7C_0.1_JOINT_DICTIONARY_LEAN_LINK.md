## Result

`E7CJointNativeDictionaryTrace.JointDictionaryEventTrace` records one row visit at a time, with explicit lookup, addition, and update results. `eventTrace_derives_dictionaryTrace` proves that such a sequence entails the existing `JointDictionaryTrace`; `eventTrace_final_is_run` then identifies its final mapping with the modeled fold. The theorem does not assume a completed `JointDictionaryTrace` or a completed final dictionary as an input.

The event relation is deliberately typed and inspectable. For each row it contains the pre-update dictionary, row, lookup result, exact sum, and post-update dictionary, with each result constrained by the corresponding abstract operation.
