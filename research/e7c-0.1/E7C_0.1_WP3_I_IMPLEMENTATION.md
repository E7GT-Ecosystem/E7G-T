# E7C/0.1 WP3-I disposable evaluator and replay checker

Status: `wp3_i_implementation_candidate_unreviewed`

Baseline: PR #38 merge `a6c562108450679e33566b8e395c4d045f042823`

Branch: `codex/e7c-01-wp3-i`

This package is a review candidate implementing the accepted bounded WP3-S
semantics and witness contract. It is deliberately disposable. Its Python data
shapes are not a stable schema or public API, and passing its tests does not
establish conformance, correctness, soundness, external truth, domain authority,
security, performance or product readiness.

## Package boundary

- `e7c_b1_evaluator.py` evaluates the accepted unary B1 term forms and emits a
  complete inline witness.
- `e7c_b1_replay_checker.py` does not import the evaluator. It validates witness
  integrity first, reruns WP2 static checking and reevaluates the term with a
  separately implemented dynamic rule engine.
- `e7c_b1_canonical.py` is the permitted shared canonical-data layer. It fixes
  canonical JSON, SHA-256 digest construction and the reviewed non-circular
  node/envelope identity bindings. It contains no evaluation control flow.
- `test_wp3_i.py` exercises the bounded implementation and adversarial cases.

## Implemented slice

The candidate covers plain and outcome-typed variables, total/strict/filtering
map tables, source-preserving views, projections, restrictions, exact finite
reconstruction and classification. Runtime interpretation is finite data only;
it contains no host-language callbacks.

Runtime values for `View` and `Projection` are checked against their declared
nominal policy, kind and source-return boundary before evaluation and replay.
Interpretation case lists must be finite functional tables. A referenced map,
view, criterion or reconstruction comparison with no exact input row is missing
replay material; it cannot become a domain failure, a negative comparison or a
successful incomplete result. In particular, a total-map row must contain its
target value and every reconstruction carrier candidate must have one view row.

The evaluator charges steps on term entry, ledger entries immediately before
append and candidates immediately before comparison. It uses stable ledger
append order, short-circuits non-success outcomes and sorts reconstruction
candidates by canonical JSON. Reconstruction succeeds only after the complete
finite carrier is examined.

## Witness and replay claim

The only accepted claim is `bounded_derivation_replay`: the supplied canonical
term and inputs replay under the pinned editions to the recorded outcome,
ordered ledger, resource progress and derivation identity. Source truth,
authenticity, authority and external-model correctness remain outside the
claim. Inline material is mandatory in this candidate; digest-only resolution
is reserved and absent material is rejected.

## Review gate

No conformance or correctness claim may be made until an exact branch head is
submitted and reviewed. WP3-I acceptance, if granted, would apply only to this
bounded disposable implementation and would not stabilize its serialization or
complete WP4 or WP7.

## Review correction after `fcab070145ae…`

The `wp3_i_revision_required` findings at the first reviewed head are addressed
by nominal runtime-view validation and complete referenced-table validation in
both implementations. Adversarial tests mutate a valid witness, replace the
claimed outcome and derivation where necessary, recompute every affected node
ID, and rebind the envelope identity before replay. They therefore demonstrate
semantic rejection rather than rejection caused only by stale integrity data.
