## What the increment supplies

The previous admission proof takes operation traces as premises. The new
adapter forwards actual `Config`, `Fraction` and `joint` calls in the unchanged
`admit` code, and the native `Joint.__init__` reached inside that unchanged
`joint` helper. Each original call executes once. The class binding inside the
helper remains the native class, so its exact-type checks keep their meaning.
Native constructor results and raw call operands are retained individually.
The actual parser list must contain those exact graph/Fraction objects, in
the observed row order. The constructor must retain the actual supplied tuple
and term objects by identity; logical tokens have strong retained references.
No expected parse, normalized Joint or serializer result is computed by the
observer. Snapshot routines supply trusted object denotation.

The Lean receipt checks each raw object/key read and call operand, registered
canonical edge sequence, tag, native graph result fields and the local rational
equation between an original signed numerator/positive denominator and the
reported native Fraction result. It constructs the existing graph, fraction,
row and row-array statement traces. All finite lists within the declared
64-row domain derive the exact `decodeJointRows` result for the actual reported
parser operand, canonical graph facts, fuel 32 and recursive unique keys.
There is no supplied decoder-result equation, completed admission trace or
extra fuel premise. Kernel checks validate these equations for fresh reported
literals; they do not prove their native provenance.

The separate Joint constructor receipt proves preservation of its reported
arity, tuple token, ordered term tokens and field literals. Its input is the
actual constructor operand **after native collection/filtering/sorting**, not
the parsed raw row list. It does not derive collection, comparator behavior,
canonical-order validation or the whole Joint normalizer equation. Native
constructor validation and dispatch remain trusted/open.

## Raw identity and observed exits

The raw fraction pair is kept separately from the reported reduced result.
A native call with `2/4` returns Fraction `1/2`; its row-prefix receipt can pass
while the later unchanged admission equality guard raises. Duplicate,
cancelled and reordered inputs also retain complete parser prefixes and their
distinct later observed exceptions. A normal empty Joint and a cancelled
nonempty raw list do not become the same admission claim.

The adapter records an observed returned/exception identity only after the
complete constructor prefix. That metadata is **outside** the Lean claim; no
host-exit classification or acceptance theorem is exported. Earlier failures,
malformed graph data and unsupported scalar shapes yield no complete packet.
Config/Fraction exceptions and evidence-limit interruptions propagate with
all temporary bindings and the capture lock restored. Pre-existing tracing,
profiling, multiple threads and overlap are refused. Runtime/source/code pins
are checked before and after; they do not prove arbitrary intervening stability,
code authenticity or universal observer completeness.

Limits bound retained rows, call boundaries and encoded evidence bytes. For n
rows there are 3n+3 recorded boundaries: two Config calls and one Fraction call
per row, the joint call, its Joint initializer and joint return. These limits
do not bound native allocation, arithmetic cost, memory peak or wall time.

## Validation gate

Require the complete 40-job Lean build, all 36 inherited audit roots plus the
new strict `[propext, Quot.sound]` audit, inherited fresh source/IR and serializer
receipts, ten fresh raw-admission prefixes and ten kernel negative controls.
The ten fixtures cover empty/signed/correlated/tag/Unicode rows, unreduced
fractions, duplicates, cancellation, reordered rows and reversed object fields.
They contain 16 raw rows and 78 call boundaries, including four observed later
admission exceptions. Forgeries change Fraction operands/results or exact-int
kind, graph fields/operands, parser order/completeness and constructor fields,
tuple/cell identity. The literal exporter preserves them for Lean to reject.

Ten focused capture tests and the adjacent admission/raw/serializer regression
are local gates. The interruption test visits all nine call-limit positions of
the two-row fixture and checks restoration before the complete boundary capture.
Local Lake is unavailable; proof build/audit/receipt validation is hosted.
Failed hosted heads retain their own verdicts in the development PR.
