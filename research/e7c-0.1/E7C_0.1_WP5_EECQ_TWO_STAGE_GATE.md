Selected source edition `E7C-EECQ-JOINT-TWO-STAGE/0.1-provisional` accepts a
binary FG3 Joint with canonical, unique exact-rational rows. The first
predicate excludes tuples whose coordinate 0 contains AB. Only its retained
Joint flows into the second predicate, which excludes tuples whose
coordinate 1 contains BC. The two fixed predicate editions are distinct.
The typed result is a three-way partition: final retained, first excluded,
second excluded. A second operation is never attempted after a first-stage
non-success. Resource limits after first success keep first exclusions in
progress and completed second exclusions as a prefix; they do not return a
partially completed success. Unsupported and undetermined outcomes remain
separate from exhaustion and malformed-input admission.

For each admitted canonical row `r = ((g₀,g₁), c)`, with exact rational `c`,
the **conservation rule** assigns exactly one destination:

1. first excluded when `AB ∈ g₀.edges`;
2. otherwise second excluded when `BC ∈ g₁.edges`;
3. otherwise final retained.

Every destination retains the entire ordered graph pair and `c` unchanged.
The three supports are disjoint and their union is the original Joint
support. No marginal product, coefficient multiplication, normalization or
unreported deletion is admitted. The executable check compares exact joint
keys and coefficients, including negative fractions. Tests use a third,
ordinary `Fraction` enumerator, equivalent-marginal but distinct correlated
Joint tables, stage-specific capability/obligation failures, both stages'
resource limits, malformed late rows and re-digested forged partitions.

The source witness includes the complete first-stage witness, both policies,
all admitted rows, ordered ledger, whole terminal and progress. Versioned IR
`E7-IR/0.6-EECQ-JOINT-TWO-STAGE-provisional` independently executes the

## Bounded proof bridge

The EEC-Q operational rule is fixed-predicate restriction over the joint
basis (§X.6.3); source implementation applies it successively to whole
correlated tuples. The finite bridge checks exact Python source witness and
independent IR observation before encoding ten cases as named Lean theorems
over `E7CB2Sequence.sequence`. Each abstract theorem checks only the
success-only control trace, prefix ledger and resource precedence after
documented value/diagnostic/event and resource-payload erasure. The first
attempt step is represented by Lean's outer step; the second attempt by its
continuation entry step. A generated manifest pins the exact source claims,
package IDs and erased traces.

predicate, proof that Python admission and exact-rational partition refine
Lean's encoded `Nat` traces, source-to-IR equivalence for all admitted
inputs, general three-way conservation in Lean, external reproduction,
profile conformance and product value. Lean remains a provisional vehicle.
No canonical, B1/B2/B3 or product migration occurs.
