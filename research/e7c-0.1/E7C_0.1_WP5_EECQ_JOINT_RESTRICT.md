## Boundary and translation

The module `adapters/eec_q_fg3_joint_b1.py` extends the provisional graph-only
target with finite homogeneous graph tuples, exact signed rational coefficients,
explicit arity, canonical collection, independent product, coordinate marginal,
FG3 graph union and `restrict_absent`. Source and target signatures compare
each component's exact graph identity and each coefficient. A joint row is one
shared choice; `independent` alone forms a Cartesian product with multiplied
coefficients. Union shares the fixed A/B/C identities and requires equal tags;
any incompatible supported pair yields a whole `domain_error`. Restriction
returns both retained and excluded states, without a strict-domain failure or
coefficient renormalisation.

The accepted fragment consists of source `joint` calls with nonempty rows of
homogeneous graph tuples, `independent` on nonempty graph states, `marginal` at
an admitted coordinate, `join(..., mode="union")` on binary graph tuples, and
graph-state `restrict_absent`. Malformed tuples are rejected before collection;
the target has no generic joint or gluing interface and does not enter quotes.
The source FG3 model rejects an empty joint constructor, including
`independent(zero, S)`; the target additionally represents an explicitly typed
zero joint with declared arity. **That extension is outside the source-model
differential fidelity claim** and requires a separate normative adequacy review
against the EEC-Q mathematical zero. It is not evidence of full FG3 conformance.

## Evidence and open obligations

Six differential/negative test methods compare correlated vs independent
choices, joint collection and marginal cancellation, union collision and
incompatible-domain failure, retained/excluded restriction, malformed tuple
admission, and the declared target-only zero boundary. The source evaluator is
imported only by tests. This is first-party evidence for the selected finite
cases, not a general proof or independent implementation.

Still open in EEC-Q WP5: pair and wire-choice gluing, quoted states and inside
execution, view vs identification, phase descent, typed E7C module integration,
resource outcomes, witnesses, proofs and a stable interface. No existing
canonical text, profile, schema, consumer or product is migrated.
