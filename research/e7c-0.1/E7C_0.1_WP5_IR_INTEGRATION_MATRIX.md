### Addendum: separately versioned EEC-Q correlated Joint restriction

The matrix edition and its **IR/0.1-var-B1** column above remain historical.
`E7-IR/0.5-EECQ-JOINT-RESTRICT-provisional` adds a separate selected
`Joint[FG3,FG3] → Outcome[Partition[Joint[FG3,FG3]],core-1]` instruction.
It pins predicate `FG3-JOINT-COORD0-ABSENT-AB/0.1-provisional`, exact rational
tuples, an explicit excluded output, complete source replay, and distinct
`resource_limit`, `unsupported` and `undetermined` outcomes. It does not
change the accepted B1 core or imply that the graph-state variable IR above
has gained general joint typing. See `E7C_0.1_WP5_EECQ_JOINT_IR_GATE.md`.

`fixtures/wp5_ir/finite_graph_var.json` is the complete, canonical JSON
reproduction package. `wp5_ir_finite_case.py` rebuilds it deterministically.
The two supplied rows contain `AB` with coefficients `1/2` and `1/2`, the
second with duplicate `AB` edge spelling. The FG3 adapter admits and collects
them to one graph `(AB, null)` with coefficient `1`. A plain Python rational
collector calculates the same graph and coefficient from the raw rows,
without calling the adapter, IR or calculus. This comparison covers **one
input**, not general FG3 equivalence, performance or fidelity.

The bridge edition `WP5-IR-FG3-SINGLE-CONFIG/0.1-provisional` selects that
graph value, registers it in the nominal `Config["Sigma-A"]` finite carrier,
and supplies the complete WP3-I fixture interpretation, map/view tables,
typed environment, external assumptions, resource policy and outcome edition.
The B1 source term is solely `var source_config`. Substitution updates every
occurrence and reorders canonical tables and retained keys. The fixture is
non-authoritative; `Sigma-A` is a test carrier, **not** a general FG3 type
translation or an assertion that a graph state is a configuration.

The source evaluator produces a terminal success and a bound witness ID. The
committed package includes the entire document, result and inline witness;
the separately coded B1 checker accepts it with no external resolver. The
provisional IR edition `E7-IR/0.1-var-B1-provisional` pins calculus, static,
dynamic, interpretation, outcome and resource editions, carries a typed
binding/carrier, canonical bytes and source-document digest, and executes an
independent one-instruction control path. IR and source agree on selected
value, empty ledger and resource progress. The IR success has
`optional_witness=null`; it does not replay or bind the source witness. The
document digest is an identity pointer, not authentication. Rebinding an IR
value with an altered carrier and fresh IDs can form a different admissible
package, so the **paired** full source package is necessary for this check.
