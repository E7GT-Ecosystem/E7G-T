Baseline: main `db4660f262505e81feb898909d45e757e12ff9cd`
(PR #111 merge). Published v0.12.1/RGP2 remains the RGP/0.1 authority;
v0.13/RWP1 and v0.14/REC1 are draft successors with RGP/0.2, not an
automatic upgrade of the finite RGP/0.1 adapter.

The digest-pinned `e7c_rgp_successor_audit.py` checks the complete X.18
subsection boundaries. R.3 (generation), R.4 (projection), R.6 (rank and
regime) and R.7 (time and history) are text-identical between v0.12.1 and
v0.13. R.0–R.2, R.5 and R.8–R.12 change; notably R.1 gains a distinct
contextual-expression family X, R.2 gains `Expresses`, and R.5.4 introduces
the legacy WPC/0.1 whole/portion relationship. All R.0–R.12 text is
identical between v0.13 and v0.14. These are **textual findings**, not
RGP/0.1→/0.2 semantic fidelity or source-program refinement results.

## Selected new judgement for the next typed implementation

The v0.13 X.18 R.2 and R.5.4.3 contract motivates a separately editioned
judgement, with source and result sorts kept distinct:

```
Γ ⊢ Fᵢ : Portion[WEdition, i, CodecEdition]
Γ ⊢ ξ : Context[role, time, regime, access, composition, inquiry]
Γ ⊢ χᵢ : ExpressionRule[Portion, Context, LocalExpression, RuleEdition, mode]
Γ ⊢ Expresses(χᵢ, Fᵢ, ξ) : Outcome[LocalExpression, ExpressionEffect]
```

Admission must validate every coordinate used by the rule, its mode
(`read_only`, `state_changing` or `generative`), portion occurrence, source
edition, authority boundary, source invariants, and the declared loss account.
The successful record must retain provenance to *that occurrence* and
context. The denotation is `χᵢ(Fᵢ, ξ)` on its declared domain, with
`unsupported`, `undetermined`, domain/admission failure and `resource_limit`
left as separate outcomes. No result inhabits `Whole` or `Portion` merely
because it is a `LocalExpression`; decoding remains a separate SR3/SR4
operation on an admitted encoded portion.

For WPC/0.1, `W : ReconstructiveWhole` and
`Y : CompositionalWhole = Compose_J((i,Fᵢ))` are distinct sorts. Any
`β_J(Y) = W` claim needs an explicit bridge, coherent edition-pinned portions
and an exact identity check. The contextual provenance triangle
`Xᵢ,ξ = χᵢ(eᵢ(W), ξ)` needs proof for its declared context and domain; it
does **not** imply `Xᵢ,ξ = Fᵢ` or `Xᵢ,ξ = W`. Two different portion occurrences
may produce the same displayed expression without becoming one occurrence.

## Counterexamples and executable coverage

1. Two individually valid SR4 portions decode to different source editions:
   each round trip may be exact, but cross-portion WPC/0.1 coherence fails.
2. Two different portions produce an equal visible string for one inquiry:
   their identities, provenance and contextual rules remain distinct.
3. A role sees a lossy local expression, while the model retains an SR4
   source portion: role access does not imply role-level exact reconstruction.
4. The v0.12.1 finite quote/host/view tests are executable *predecessor*
   fixtures. A version-pinned RGP/0.2 `Expresses` evaluator and independent
   source differential are **absent**; this is an explicit completion gap.

Next implementation gate: define the typed v0.13/v0.14 expression rule with
an immutable complete context, finite exact source/portion identity and
distinct outcome constructors. Run one bounded example and all three
counterexamples against an independent interpreter, then prove its
admission, provenance and loss observations against a Lean rule. Complete
the remaining changed RGP clauses and RGP/0.1→/0.2 differential before
claiming full RGP/0.2 fidelity. WPC-Core/0.2 and its optional Evolution and
Distributed modules have their own separate gates; this document does not
give those modules coverage.
