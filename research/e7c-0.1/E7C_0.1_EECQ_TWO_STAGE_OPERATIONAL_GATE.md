# EEC-Q two-stage operational relation (provisional exact-head review)

The pure all-list mathematical layer was merged in PR #102 (merge commit
`fb51c34a33ae2cbbbfb73663b8b3e1218541cbb9`). Published experimental
v0.12.1/RGP2 remains the source; the selected predicate, source and IR
editions remain those of PR #101.

`plan` is the complete specification trace. Its second-stage events cover
**all** first-stage retained rows, including rows later excluded by the second
predicate. The earlier use of the *final* retained partition here was wrong.
`run` instead executes a cursor with unvisited rows. At each attempt or row,
it checks fuel, charges a step, checks ledger capacity, and only then advances
the cursor. A full ledger consumes the attempted step and appends no event.
A zero-step run does not visit a row. Resource termination never returns a
partial `Partition`.

The machine records `secondStarted` when the second attempt's step is
charged, before trying the ledger append. Thus it remains true if that
append fails. A one-row fixture fixes the case with step bound 4 and ledger
bound 2: two first-stage events are appended, the second attempt charges
step 3 without an event, and the terminal is `resourceLimit`. A general
failed-second-append Lean boundary law accompanies the fixture.

**Lean all-input claims in this package:**

- `future_start_is_plan` and `run_ledger_matches_plan` connect the staged
  cursor's actual events to the independent complete plan for every policy,
  finite list and budget.
- `sufficient_budgets_produce_partition` proves that both bounds covering the
  complete ready-policy plan yield #102's exact three-way `source` partition,
  retaining complete correlated graph pairs and rational coefficients.
- `exhaustion_returns_prefix_and_steps` proves a short budget yields only
  `resourceLimit`, the exact plan prefix, and the failed-append extra step
  when ledger space is exhausted after step charge.
- `drive_progress_fields`, `all_stage_first_progress`, and
  `exhaustion_exact_progress` establish the full progress record: ordered
  ledger, first exclusions only after every first-stage event is appended,
  exact second-exclusion prefix, and charged steps. The first exclusion
  contents agree with #102's mathematical partition.

The Python tests independently enumerate finite source replay and IR
observations under budgets and policies; the named failed-second-append
fixture complements that enumeration. Lean tests also cover second-stage
excluded rows, success, both stage limits, and policy stops. Exact-head Lean
build, axiom audit, source/IR replay, and adversarial review are required
before merging; successful CI alone is not semantic acceptance.
