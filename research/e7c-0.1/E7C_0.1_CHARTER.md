# E7C/0.1 charter — neutral WP1 draft

Status: `drafted_unfrozen`; this document records vocabulary boundaries only.
It does not stabilise syntax, grammar, IR, runtime, schema or API.

## Purpose

Investigate whether a small typed constructive substrate can express bounded
fragments of existing E7G-T profiles through explicit adapters while
preserving their identities, outcomes, failures, loss, dependence and history.

## Non-goals

- replacing or amending v0.12.1;
- merging every profile into one algebra;
- proving general consistency, completeness or novelty;
- supplying quantum, security, legal, medical or empirical truth semantics;
- creating a stable implementation interface during WP0/WP1.

## Unfrozen judgement families

Future notation must distinguish formation/typing, admission, evaluation,
definitional equality, model-relative denotational equality, observational
equivalence and inquiry-relative phase equivalence. The sample notation in the
mandate is illustrative; ADR-001–004 and ADR-011 remain open.

## Candidate semantic families

Entity/configuration, exact formal state, dependence-preserving family, typed
map/rule, quote, view, source-retaining encoding, whole/portion, scope diagram,
phase, history, claim/evidence/status, witness and explicit outcome are
candidate families. Inclusion in this list is not admission to the nucleus.

## Outcomes and effects

The candidate terminal tags are success, invalid input, type error, domain
error, unsupported, undetermined and resource limit. Empty, zero and no-source
fibres remain typed values only where their codomain admits them. Candidate
effect dimensions are partiality, loss, inquiry, access, authority,
alternatives, evidence, history, resources and external bridges. Their carrier
and composition remain open under ADR-004.

## Equality boundary

Definitional equality alone may eventually support decidable conversion.
Denotational equality is relative to a pinned model; observational and phase
relations are relative to declared criteria. No reverse implication or use as
general substitutability is assumed.

## Core admission and boundedness

A primitive requires two conservative uses or an indispensable term-language
role. Otherwise it belongs in a derived library or profile adapter. Abstract
mathematics may describe symbolic structures; E7C-B1, if later authorised,
must remain finite/resource-bounded with explicit non-success outcomes.

## Theorem inventory, not claims

Future reviewed statements are required for substitution, canonicalisation,
determinism, type and effect preservation, declared progress, composition,
bounded reconstruction soundness/completeness, quotient descent, witness
replay, adapter fidelity and lowering adequacy. Every item is presently
`not_started`; tests cannot promote proof status.

## Compatibility posture

Adapters will be classified as faithful embedding, faithful interpretation
with explicit named loss/quotient, partial translation or unsupported. No
adapter, product migration or executable slice is authorised by this charter.
