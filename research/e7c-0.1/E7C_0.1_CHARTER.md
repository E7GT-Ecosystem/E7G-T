# E7C/0.1 charter — bounded WP1 review candidate

Status: `wp1_formal_vocabulary_drafted_unfrozen`

Authority: accepted WP0 control surface at merge `64c49b4676…`

Canonical baseline: E7G-T v0.12.1-experimental, `RGP/0.1`, revision RGP2

This charter proposes one typed constitutional substrate for several preserved
mathematical theories. It is not a grammar, calculus implementation, canonical
kernel amendment, or claim that every source profile has a faithful embedding.

## 1. Purpose

E7C/0.1 investigates whether bounded fragments of E7G-T can share:

- formation, typing, admission and outcome disciplines;
- explicit effects, loss and resource obligations;
- separated equality and equivalence relations;
- source-relative module and adapter contracts;
- theorem and witness obligations suitable for later external metatheory.

The specialised mathematics of EEC-Q, SF, CFS, RGP, WPC, REC and MSC remains
in versioned libraries or adapters unless the core-admission rule is met.

## 2. Non-goals

WP1 does not:

- replace, amend or promote v0.12.1 or any successor;
- choose a concrete grammar, parser representation, IR, runtime or API;
- merge formal states, families, evidence states and compatible diagrams;
- define product-specific quantum, security, storage or domain semantics;
- establish consistency, completeness, decidability, novelty or effectiveness;
- authorize WP2/WP3 or executable construction.

## 3. Metalanguage and object-language boundary

WP1 uses ordinary typed mathematics—sets, indexed families, relations, partial
maps and finite structures—as an external metalanguage. Symbols in the formal
vocabulary name candidate object-language judgements and sorts; they are not a
stabilised concrete syntax. Initial soundness must be established externally or
in a proof assistant before controlled self-description is attempted.

## 4. Constitutional judgement families

The proposed judgement classes are specified in
`E7C_0.1_FORMAL_VOCABULARY.md`. They separately cover:

1. signature, type and context formation;
2. obligation admission;
3. typed terms with static effect bounds;
4. value/input admission;
5. bounded evaluation to a terminal outcome and runtime ledger;
6. definitional equality;
7. model-relative denotational equality;
8. observation-relative equivalence;
9. inquiry-relative phase equivalence;
10. modules, adapters and fidelity dispositions.

No observational, phase, representation or reconstruction relation licenses
type conversion.

## 5. Candidate type families

WP1 admits a typed vocabulary, not a final primitive set. Candidate nucleus
families include declarations/editions, entities, configurations, descriptions,
typed maps, source-linked views, encodings, histories, terminal outcomes,
effect ledgers and witnesses. Exact formal states, symbolic families,
family-of-state nesting, recursive generative structures, whole–portion
constitutions, evidence states and multi-scope diagrams remain profile or
derived-library families pending conservative adapters.

Every candidate operation has a typed domain and codomain in the vocabulary.
No operation over an undifferentiated `object` or `value` universe is admitted.

## 6. Effects and outcomes

Static effects bound possible dependence or obligations; runtime ledgers record
what was actually incurred. Candidate dimensions are partiality, loss,
inquiry, access, authority, alternatives, evidence, history, resources and
external bridges. Their algebra remains open under ADR-004.

The outcome taxonomy preserves `success`, `type_error`, `domain_error`,
`unsupported`, `undetermined` and `resource_limit`. Whether `invalid_input`
belongs outside evaluation or is an operational outcome remains ADR-011. Empty
families, zero formal states and empty reconstruction fibres are typed values,
not interchangeable failures.

## 7. Equality ladder

The charter requires distinct relations for exact/canonical identity,
definitional equality, model-relative denotational equality, isomorphism,
observational equivalence, phase equivalence, representation agreement,
reconstruction compatibility and empirical similarity.

Only reviewed definitional equality may eventually drive conversion. It must be
sound in every admitted denotational model. No converse implication is assumed.
Lossy translations must name the quotient or abstraction relation that licenses
their collapse.

## 8. Core and module admission

A proposed nucleus primitive requires either:

1. two independent conservative source uses without semantic distortion; or
2. a demonstrated indispensable role in the term language.

Otherwise it belongs to a derived library, a profile adapter, or the
unsupported set. A module exports typed families, operations, outcomes,
effects, equations and observations under an edition pin. Import never grants
canonical status, authority, access or truth.

## 9. Abstract calculus and E7C-B1 boundary

The abstract calculus may denote symbolic or countable structures only when
their mathematics is explicit. A later E7C-B1 implementation must be finite or
explicitly bounded and must distinguish complete success from
`undetermined`/`resource_limit`. Successful exhaustive reconstruction means the
exact fibre for the declared finite carrier and policy.

## 10. Theorem inventory

WP1 records statements to be formalised later; every item remains
`not_started`: formation decidability for a selected fragment, substitution,
definitional-equality soundness, deterministic evaluation, type preservation,
effect/ledger soundness for every terminal outcome, declared progress,
composition, bounded reconstruction soundness and completeness, quotient
descent, witness replay soundness, adapter preservation/reflection and lowering
adequacy. Tests are not proofs.

## 11. Compatibility posture

Each adapter must be classified as `faithful_embedding`,
`faithful_interpretation_with_explicit_loss`, `partial_translation`, or
`unsupported`. It must pin source/profile editions, preserve typing,
admission, outcomes and failures, and state which source distinctions it
reflects. REC adapters additionally bind provenance/dependence groups, source,
model edition and decision policy.

## 12. WP1 completion boundary

WP1 may pass only after formal review finds no untyped proposed core primitive
and no conflated equality. Passing WP1 authorizes specification work at the
next separately approved gate; it does not itself authorize WP2/WP3,
implementation, IR/runtime/API stabilization, canonical promotion or product
migration.
