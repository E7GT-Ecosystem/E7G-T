## Compatibility conclusion

`retain` preserves an existing source and status; it does not place the item in
the E7C nucleus. `adapt` authorises only later construction of a separately
identified adapter after its semantics are reviewed. Existing products retain
their native identities, bytes and APIs. No row authorises `replace` or
`retire`, and no consumer is automatically migrated.
