The target module `adapters/eec_q_fg3_assembly_b1.py` adds exact graph-pair
`Assembly` identities and an `AssemblyState` separate from graph `State`.
`join_pair` consumes a binary `Joint` of graph tuples; `join_wire_choice`
consumes a ternary `Joint` whose third graph must have no edges and a `uses`
or `contains` tag. The relation, left and right graph identities and exact
signed rational coefficients are retained. Outputs with equal *full*
assembly identities collect and may cancel. A joint row is one correlated
choice; no independent product or coefficient multiplication is inferred by
the join itself. Independent product remains an explicit prior operation.

For admitted graph tuples, both modes compare against the source FG3 `join`
evaluator. Pair mode is total on the selected graph-pair carrier. Invalid
wiring in any **supported** triple makes wire-choice fail as a whole with a
typed `domain_error`; FG3 raises `DomainError` for the same case. A malformed
wiring that was admitted as a graph but cancelled at the joint-construction
stage is no longer a supported branch and is not executed. The target rejects
wrong joint arity at admission; FG3's untyped empty joint has no stored arity,
so a source empty join needs a separately declared target arity.

This is FG3's fixed A/B/C identity and graph-only operand fragment. It does
not support assemblies inside assemblies, box operands, generic interfaces,
arbitrary link rewriting, declared relation port typing, unrestricted gluing
or a stable public syntax. The target is an independent provisional profile
module outside the accepted E7C-B1 typed/evaluated core. Six differential and
negative test methods cover correlated versus independent pairs, wire
alternatives, strict failure, cancellation before a partial join, exact
assembly identity and rejection of incorrect arity/host coercions.
