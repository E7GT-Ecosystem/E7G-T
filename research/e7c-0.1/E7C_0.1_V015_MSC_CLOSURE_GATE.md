`E7CMSCFiniteClosure.lean` models one bounded subset of §X.21 MSC.3. A
candidate is an already formed assignment of one state per scope. Each
heterogeneously typed link contains two **total** maps into its own common
comparison carrier with exact equality. `check_iff` proves its executable
link scan equals the mathematical conjunction for every candidate and every
finite link list. `selected_iff` proves the filtered assignments are exactly
the globally compatible candidates, preserving complete assignments and
input order. `incompatible_iff` and `singleton_sound` identify absence and
uniqueness relative to that enumeration. The classifier distinguishes
unavailable maps, an empty evaluable carrier, a resource bound exceeded
before scanning, incompatible, unique and ambiguous. `undetermined` is a
distinct output sort reserved for a future admitted decision procedure;
this finite exact subset never returns it. An empty evaluable carrier is
`unsupported` per MSC.3. The older Boolean parity-cycle theorem supplies
a link-wise satisfiable but globally incompatible witness.

The independent `adapters/msc_finite_b1.py` enumerates total exact-map
diagrams, and `adapters/test_msc_finite_b1.py` compares its outcomes to the
pinned MSC-B1 source fixture, including obstruction and resource/unsupported
paths. These are bounded differential observations, **not** a proof of Python
execution against the Lean model. The Lean theorem presupposes a complete
candidate enumeration with no duplicate assignments, admitted total
well-typed maps, and a common-codomain encoding. The correspondence between
those premises and the Python source/adapter admission, product enumeration,
evaluation order and serialization is unproved. General partial maps,
non-equality criteria, fibres, access classes, undetermined procedures and
other MSC operations are outside this theorem. Full E7C is incomplete;
the EEC-Q actual Python/IR-to-Lean link is also still open.

Affected consumers: E7C research and future opt-in v0.15 users. Existing
source pins, product IDs, APIs, schemas and fixtures are retained. This is
an `adapt` calculus bridge for MSC-B1 at a newly explicit v0.15 source pin,
with no product migration or conformance promotion.
