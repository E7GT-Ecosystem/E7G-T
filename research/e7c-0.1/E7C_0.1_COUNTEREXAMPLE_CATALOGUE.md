## Catalogue rules

1. A counterexample stays recorded after a bug is fixed; the rejected
   proposition may otherwise return under a new implementation.
2. `executable evidence` names a finite regression, not a universal proof.
3. Deferred entries cannot justify a new nucleus constructor before the exact
   source fragment and failed derivation attempt are recorded.
4. New counterexamples must identify the affected theorem, source/profile
   edition, compatibility impact and whether the theorem is narrowed,
   withdrawn or left open.
