This independently coded adapter accepts canonical finite rational `Points`
domains of up to 32 points, exact untagged FG3 graph states and canonical
polynomials of degree at most two. The outer family consists of parameter
assignments; its members are inner EEC-Q states. Polynomial coefficients
apply only **inside** each state. No parameter membership is converted into
an EEC coefficient. The admission fragment is narrower than the source's
intervals, radicals, arbitrary supported degree and point normalisation;
these inputs require another reviewed translation.

`shared_union` reuses one outer parameter and computes inner formal state
products pointwise. A separate, locally defined `independent_pairs` result
uses two parameters and a product assignment carrier; it cannot be passed
to `shared_union`. The latter comparison operation is **not an exported CG3
source API**. Exact realisation scans the declared finite points and returns
`EMPTY`, `UNIQUE`, `AMBIGUOUS` or `RESOURCE_LIMIT`, never a partial success.
The empty outer family differs from a nonempty family whose every inner
state is the zero formal state.

Four differential test methods compare the pinned source CFS model's
symbolic coefficients, pointwise instantiated states, shared composition,
finite no-objective realisation and empty versus zero outcomes. A separate
cross-assignment witness demonstrates why shared parameters must not be
silently treated as independent. Context, graph tag, canonical point order,
polynomial degree and edition are checked before the selected operation.

This is a bounded partial translation and not an E7C-B1 grammar addition,
proof of general SF/CFS conformance, general solver, continuous family
representation, quantum interpretation or product adoption. It changes no
canonical source, IDs, data, API or consumer. Next WP5 coverage gap is the
finite RGP adapter, then versioned calculus-level typing, semantics and
proof obligations for admitted profile operations before E7-IR promotion.
