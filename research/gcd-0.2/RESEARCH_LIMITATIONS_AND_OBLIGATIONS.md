# GCD/0.2 — Open research obligations and stop conditions

## Established only within the named finite carrier

The Python evaluator defines three registered workflow signatures, 11 admitted configurations total, finite explicit families, two registered generators, one explicit singleton-seeding adapter, a supplied/policy choice record, replayable operation receipts, a model-only authorisation fixture, and a finite rank-linked lineage constructor. Exhaustive enumeration proves the stated equations for those enumerated inputs **relative to the implemented definitions**, subject to implementation correctness. Tests are first-party implementation checks, not independent formal proof or conformance of the complete v0.15 calculus.

## Open mathematical obligations

1. **General constitutive test:** prove that a proposed material semantic extension is nontrivial under a principled semantic identity contract, not merely declared so by the registry. Investigate conservative extensions, observational equivalence and the role of definability and interpretation functors.
2. **General comparison carriers:** define and validate equivalence and strict refinement on partially defined maps, constrained families, mixed source/target signatures and possibly infinite symbolic carriers. Establish a consistent treatment of unavailable comparison predicates.
3. **Semantic-identity governance:** the present bounded model hardcodes which kinds and relations are semantic. Independent reviewers must evaluate whether this is a suitable foundation for more general novelty claims and how arbitrary schema change is prevented from certifying vacuous novelty.
4. **Expansive generation with constraints:** formalise embeddings when old alternatives cannot be preserved pointwise, or when transitions change admissibility, domain rules, semantic interpretations or signatures. Classify lossy and non-expansive generation with fully typed outcomes.
5. **Generator selection:** implement finite typed `Family[GenRule]`, support heterogeneous source/target signatures, rule application and rule-choice provenance without self-application or arbitrary code execution.
6. **SF and CFS adapters:** implement actual edition-pinned adapters to the v0.15 symbolic model with sound membership and shared dependence. Handle overlapping assignments and infinite denotations without confusing witness existence with general decidability.
7. **Choice-act audit:** design an authenticated, append-only occurrence model if external human choices or authorisations are claimed. A SHA-256 digest and replay do not prove historic origin, actor identity, time or non-repudiation.
8. **WPC-Evolution bridge:** add externally reviewed coverage, proposal identity, causal parents, policy, reconciliation, commitment and refresh. Current `CommitModel` is not that bridge.
9. **History and noncommutation:** define general typed operation-order comparison including partial failures, differing output sorts, shared choices, contextual criteria and access projections. The provided counterexample establishes only the finite model's selected distinction.
10. **Recursion and limits:** generalise the lineage constructor to genuinely packed typed finite payloads; prove rank monotonicity and admissibility. Current parent-digest chain establishes at most reference lineage (SR0-like), not exact recursive self-encoding or SR4 recovery.
11. **Potential infinite limits:** if pursued, specify a mathematical carrier, connecting maps, compatible-family/colimit or inverse-limit assumptions and topology where relevant; do not assert existence of a completed limit object from repeated bounded tests alone.
12. **Independent formal review:** have a separate researcher or implementation verify the definitions and either reproduce or refute the exhaustive finite claims; document counterexamples rather than editing the original run silently.
13. **Independent implementation/replay:** produce a second implementation of the same versioned interchange, validate canonicalisation and identity, and compare results across implementations and independent environments.
14. **Practical baseline:** benchmark against carefully engineered ordinary workflow/state-machine/version-control schemas, not a deliberately impoverished straw-man baseline. Measure what independent controls GCD adds, error prevention, audit effort, latency and engineering cost.
15. **Versioned schemas:** freeze a normative external interchange format with field constraints, resource policy, hashes, canonicalisation algorithm, compatibility and migration before any product adoption.

## Explicit non-claims

- No established new mathematical theorem beyond bounded deductions from stipulated structures.
- No independent reproduction, external-world truth, universal mathematical completeness, general infinite hierarchy, or physical-system correspondence.
- No secure identity authentication, non-repudiation, safe production commitment, execution authorisation or actual external state change.
- No general arbitrary signature implementation, general symbolic/continuous family solver, actual EEC-Q/SF/CFS/RGP/WPC/REC/MSC runtime compatibility or universal GCD conformance.
- No demonstrated speed, accuracy, commercial superiority or uniqueness over mature workflow and verification technology.

## Promotion and stop criteria

**STOP** a stronger claim if the semantic new-kind witness is purely observational; the grounding map or preservation embedding is undefined; the novelty comparison cannot be decided; the generation rule changes earlier alternatives without a loss account; a choice cannot prove exact membership; actor/authority status is merely asserted; the selected source family edition is stale; the receipt fails replay; or an allegedly exhaustive check exceeds the stated carrier.

**PROMOTION GATE:** independently reviewed equations and identity rules; fully versioned schema; independent checker and reproduction; positive/negative conformance; one externally meaningful consumer compared with a strong standard baseline; source/profile migration record; explicit written promotion decision. Until then, preserve the research isolation and canonical pins.
