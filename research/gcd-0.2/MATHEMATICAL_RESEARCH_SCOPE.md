# GCD/0.2 — Mathematical research scope

**Status:** editorial scope statement for the non-canonical GCD/0.2 package. This document is not a new mathematical axiom, rule or profile release.

GCD is a finite, typed mathematical investigation into semantic-signature refinement, explicitly admitted possibility families, grounding maps, criterion-relative generation, selected family members, choice-act records and replayable witnesses. Its claims are conditional on the specified carriers, admission criteria, exact identity, operations and finite-domain checks.

The terms **differentiation**, **generation**, **choice** and **constitution** are technical terms within declared mathematical models. They do not, without an explicitly supplied external model and independently validated bridge, denote an observation or event outside the model.

Historical motivations are not mathematical premises and are excluded from current-facing mathematical documentation. Earlier wording remains accessible in Git history for provenance and audit; it is not retained as an active research appendix.

The repository records a separate first-party reference evaluator and bounded exhaustive tests. Its implementation and mathematical claims have not been independently validated. Neither source versioning nor a passing finite test suite proves a general theorem or a stronger real-world effect.
