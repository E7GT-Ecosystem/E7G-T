# E7G-T Ecosystem Operating Kernel

## Portfolio Constitution, Development Instruction and Commercial Direction

## 0. Purpose

This document instructs any human or AI system assisting with an E7G-T-family project to develop it as part of a coherent, ambitious and commercially significant technology ecosystem.

It is a **proposed ecosystem operating kernel**, not a replacement for the E7G-T Unified Geometry-Thinking Kernel. E7G-T remains the authoritative conceptual, constructive and reasoning source. This proposal is pinned to E7G-T v0.15-experimental-MSC1 at the exact commit, blob and SHA-256 named in the front matter. v0.15 is the experimental canonical source for new work only if this proposal is accepted. v0.12.1/RGP2, v0.13/RWP1 and v0.14/REC1 remain immutable historical editions; products and artifacts retain their original pins until a reviewed migration. E7G-T v0.11-UC5 remains the inherited constitutional predecessor and compatibility baseline.

This document governs how projects derived from, informed by, or interoperating with E7G-T are selected, structured, developed, validated, protected, connected and commercialised. It prevents both ceremonial alignment claims and the inference that every product must implement every profile in the source.

The portfolio objective is not to accumulate small utilities. It is to create a family of independently valuable, high-end systems that can also combine into a larger architecture for evidence-aware computing, trustworthy transformation and controlled reliance.

The commercial ambition includes products and platforms capable, if validated and successfully adopted, of generating millions, tens of millions or potentially hundreds of millions in revenue or enterprise value. This ambition sets the scale of markets and problems to pursue. It does not constitute evidence that any product will achieve such outcomes.

## 1. Instruction to the receiving AI or development team

You are assisting with one project inside the **E7G-T family ecosystem**.

You MUST:

1. read the exact pinned current E7G-T kernel before materially redefining the project;
2. identify the project's specific user, buyer, problem, protected invariants and evidence requirements;
3. preserve the distinction between conceptual source, software implementation, empirical evidence and commercial claim;
4. design the project to be independently useful and commercially defensible;
5. identify reusable ecosystem capabilities rather than duplicating them within each product;
6. preserve interoperability without forcing every product into one monolithic codebase;
7. treat integrations, partnerships, grants and pilots as means of validation and market entry, not as substitutes for a viable product;
8. distinguish present capability from planned capability and strategic possibility;
9. challenge technically weak, commercially implausible or legally unsafe assumptions;
10. select the next move that creates the greatest credible increase in technical evidence, market access, defensibility or strategic leverage;
11. maintain a repository-local kernel-alignment record before beginning a material milestone;
12. classify existing implementation as `retain`, `adapt`, `replace` or `retire` against the distinctions in the exact pinned source that can change program meaning;
13. review every product against EEC-Q, SF, CFS, RGP, WPC, MSC and REC, even when the selected disposition is `not_applicable`;
14. implement only the profiles and capabilities that materially improve the product's declared task; record capabilities, limitations, evidence and migration posture in its build map.

You MUST NOT:

- describe ambition as validated market demand;
- treat a prototype, test, benchmark, conversation, NDA, application, letter of intent or partner interest as a completed commercial outcome;
- claim that a derivative product proves E7G-T as mathematics, science or universal theory;
- attach the E7G-T name to an ordinary product without a material and inspectable relationship;
- surrender reusable ecosystem IP merely to secure one pilot or funding opportunity;
- make a project dependent on one vendor, collaborator, hardware platform or funding body unless that dependency is explicit and strategically justified;
- conceal uncertainty, negative results, failed tests, lossy transformations or limitations for promotional convenience;
- build a comprehensive platform where a smaller falsifiable implementation can test the central proposition first.
- describe an inherited or product-local workflow as v0.15-aligned without an inspectable mapping to relevant v0.15 concepts, profile boundaries, capabilities and limitations;
- treat EEC-Q coefficients as probabilities, evidence weights, confidence values, security decisions, storage deletion semantics or quantum amplitudes;
- continue a material milestone when its current data model irreversibly collapses distinctions required by its declared future profile route.

## 3. Ecosystem thesis

The common ecosystem proposition is:

> Consequential systems should preserve an inspectable account of what was modelled, how it was transformed, what was preserved or lost, what evidence was produced, and what reliance that evidence permits.

The ecosystem seeks to make this proposition operational across computation, data, security, storage, AI, quantum systems and other evidence-critical environments.

The shared value is not an abstract claim to truth. It is the practical ability to reduce risks arising from:

- source/representation confusion;
- silent information loss;
- ambiguous reconstruction;
- unverifiable transformation;
- broken provenance;
- unsupported inference;
- unjustified certainty;
- temporal overgeneralisation;
- context drift;
- incompatible equivalence criteria;
- hidden boundary crossings;
- claims stronger than their supporting evidence.

## 5. Independence and interoperability rule

Every E7G-T-family product MUST satisfy both conditions:

### 5.1 Independent value

The product must solve a defined problem for a defined user or buyer without requiring adoption of the entire ecosystem.

### 5.2 Ecosystem leverage

The product should become materially more valuable when connected to other ecosystem components through shared primitives, interfaces or evidence paths.

Avoid two failure modes:

1. **fragmentation** — unrelated products share only an E7 label; and
2. **monolithisation** — every product depends on an unfinished universal platform.

Use modular contracts, schemas, adapters, receipts and conformance profiles. Shared interfaces should be stable enough for interoperability while allowing independent release schedules and technology choices.

The ecosystem distinguishes a **shared contract/conformance layer**, which is required early, from a **shared execution runtime**, which remains conditional. The former pins identities, sorts, outcomes and compatibility fixtures. The latter may be extracted only after two independent consumers demonstrate reuse without semantic distortion.

## 7. Product admission test

A proposed project belongs in the E7G-T family only if it passes the following test.

### 7.1 Material relationship

At least one E7G-T discipline must change the product's architecture, semantics, validation behaviour or decision controls in a material and testable way.

### 7.2 Consequential problem

The project must address a problem whose failure has meaningful economic, operational, security, scientific, legal, safety or reputational consequences.

### 7.3 Identifiable buyer

There must be a plausible organisation or role with budget authority and a reason to pay. Users, beneficiaries and buyers must not be silently conflated.

### 7.4 Measurable mechanism

The project must specify what it does, what baseline it improves upon and what observation could show that it failed.

### 7.5 Defensibility

The project should develop at least one durable advantage: proprietary implementation, specialised know-how, unique evidence, integration depth, conformance ecosystem, data advantage, regulatory fit, switching cost, patentable invention or trusted standard position.

### 7.6 Portfolio leverage

The project must either reuse an ecosystem capability or create one that another credible family product could reuse.

If these conditions are not met, classify the work as an experiment, internal tool, research note or non-family project rather than inflating its status.

### Required seven-profile alignment record

Every product completes this review, regardless of which profiles it later implements. A product MAY implement only the capabilities that materially improve its declared task.

| Profile family | Minimum review question | Scope caveat to record |
|---|---|---|
| EEC-Q | Does the task require exact signed rational combinations, correlated Joint identity, cancellation or explicit restriction? | Coefficients are not probabilities, evidence weights or quantum amplitudes; bounded adapters do not imply full calculus conformance. |
| SF | Does the task require symbolic families, parameterised alternatives or realisation? | Finite points do not establish general solver, realisation or failure correspondence. |
| CFS | Does the task require nested family/state composition or pointwise transformations? | Record nesting order and distinguish symbolic outer families from exact inner combinations. |
| RGP | Does the task depend on reciprocal whole/part structure, rank, generation, history or access? | Preserve RGP/0.1 historical pins separately from RGP/0.2; a finite encoding is not general whole/part conformance. |
| WPC | Does the task depend on constitution, membership, ambiguity, retrieval, evolution or distribution? | Keep WPC versions and subprofiles separate; WPC Distributed remains unsupported unless a runtime and declared fault/trust/authority model are evidenced. |
| MSC | Does the task require coherence across scopes, carriers, maps or criteria? | Finite total-map closure does not establish partial-map, fibre, obstruction, infinite or external-domain conformance. |
| REC | Does the task require typed evidence-to-claim admission, conflict retention, modality/time scope or replayable witnesses? | Bounded information-flow replay is not source truth, domain validity, authority, calibrated uncertainty or improved AI performance. |

## 7.2 Product build-map requirements

Every material product build map MUST record more than a kernel version. At minimum it records:

- exact source edition, profile revision, commit, source path, Git blob and SHA-256;
- all seven profile reviews and their rationale, including `not_applicable` decisions;
- task-level capabilities sought and implemented, plus unsupported behavior and explicit non-claims;
- evidence linked to exact code, fixture, test, theorem or external artifact heads, with commands, results, scope and reviewer/independence status;
- component-level `retain`, `adapt`, `replace` or `retire` posture;
- preserved and affected IDs, APIs, schemas, data, edition pins and consumers;
- compatibility and migration checks, reversible steps or rollback posture, and unresolved semantic links;
- the next falsifiable gate and what result would block or revise the migration.

A source-version field without these mappings is an inventory entry, not an alignment or build map. Historical build maps remain immutable; successors link them and add a dated migration record rather than rewriting their claims.

Priority environments MAY include:

- quantum computing and quantum sensing;
- AI assurance and autonomous systems;
- cybersecurity and protected infrastructure;

- government, scientific and critical-infrastructure computing.

Market selection MUST be based on accessible evidence, buyer pain, procurement reality, competition, regulatory conditions, integration cost and credible willingness to pay—not market size alone.

- high-value platform contracts;
- private or sovereign deployments;

Low-cost utilities MAY be used as adoption channels, test beds or developer tools. They should not consume the portfolio unless they provide strategic leverage toward the high-value system.

## 9. Development sequence

Each product should normally progress through the following gates.

### Gate 0 — Thesis

Define the problem, affected party, consequence, E7G-T relationship and proposed mechanism.

### Gate 1 — Falsifiable specification

Define inputs, outputs, boundaries, protected properties, baseline, metrics, failure criteria and non-claims.

### Gate 1A — Kernel alignment and migration posture

Before material implementation, pin the exact E7G-T source and complete a seven-profile alignment review for EEC-Q, SF, CFS, RGP, WPC, MSC and REC. For each profile, record task relevance, disposition (`adopted`, `experimental`, `deferred` or `not_applicable`), capability sought, current implementation, limitations and non-claims, evidence, and migration posture. `not_applicable` requires a task-based rationale; it is not an unreviewed default. Map relevant native objects and operations, or state why no mapping applies. Classify each affected component as `retain`, `adapt`, `replace` or `retire`. Implement only profile capabilities that materially help the product's task. Test relevant hazards, including source/view confusion, hidden restriction, lost joint dependence, collapsed failure outcomes, invalid cancellation, quotation distribution, operation-order loss, unsupported distributed assumptions, source-truth overclaims and model-relative coherence overclaims.

Passing Gate 1A does not mean the product implements v0.15 or all seven profiles. It means the profile families were all considered and the product has an explicit, reviewable, evidence-bounded relationship to relevant parts of the pinned source without unknowingly closing a migration path.

### Gate 2 — Minimal working artifact

Implement the smallest system capable of testing the distinctive mechanism.

### Gate 3 — Internal conformance

Provide tests, reproducible fixtures, versioning, traceability and documented limitations.

### Gate 4 — External evidence

Test with a real external artifact, workflow, user, partner, dataset or hardware system. Preserve negative and null results.

### Gate 5 — Buyer validation

Determine whether an identifiable buyer recognises the problem, values the result and can support a pilot or purchase.

### Gate 6 — Protected scaling

Harden architecture, security, operations, licensing, IP position and integration strategy before broad commercial exposure.

### Gate 7 — Ecosystem integration

Connect the validated product to relevant E7 components through explicit contracts. Do not integrate merely for architectural elegance.

Funding MAY enter at any gate when it accelerates the next credible transition. Funding MUST NOT be treated as evidence that later gates have been passed.

- **FAILED** — did not meet a declared criterion.

Passing one status does not imply a later status.

## 11. E7Q-IR strategic instruction

E7Q-IR is the working name for the proposed evidence-native quantum intermediate representation. It should initially be positioned as interoperable with OpenQASM and provider-specific execution formats, not as an established replacement for them.

E7Q-IR SHOULD:

- remain vendor-, hardware- and front-end-neutral;
- accept Hieroglyphs as a valuable first integration without depending exclusively on it;
- import and export existing quantum representations where feasible;
- bind source identity, transformations, hardware context, observations and claims;
- make preservation, loss, equivalence and provenance machine-inspectable;
- support E7Q verification receipts;
- permit E7G-T trace construction across the complete computational path;
- distinguish algorithmic lowering, device compilation, scheduling, calibration, execution and interpretation;
- prevent a successful structural check from being represented as hardware superiority or quantum advantage without the required evidence.
- preserve dependent candidate choices as joint or constrained families rather than reconstructing them from independent marginals;
- distinguish a source-preserving view from identification and restriction;
- record transformation domain, order, preservation, loss and failure as part of the executable history;
- package complete compilation constructions and receipts through immutable, versioned boundaries where useful.

The first objective is not a complete programming language. It is a minimal, executable and verifiable IR plus conformance tooling demonstrated on existing QFT and QEC evidence packages.

## 12. Collaboration and joint-product discipline

Collaborators may contribute languages, compilers, hardware access, datasets, domain expertise, distribution or capital. Collaboration does not automatically merge ownership.

Before creating a joint product, establish in writing:

- each party's background IP;
- independently developed improvements;
- joint foreground IP;
- ownership and licensing of specifications, code, data, models, tests and documentation;
- use of names, marks and attribution;
- confidentiality and publication rights;
- patent-sensitive disclosure procedures;
- commercial territories and fields of use, if any;
- responsibility for costs, liabilities and support;
- decision rights;
- exit, continuation and post-termination rights.

An NDA protects confidential disclosure but normally does not settle these matters. A separate development, IP and commercial agreement is required before material joint-product work.

Avoid unnecessary exclusivity. Any exclusivity must be narrow, time-bounded, performance-conditioned and justified by equivalent strategic value.

## 13. IP, licensing and publication

Maintain an explicit boundary among:

1. the public E7G-T specification;
2. open-source implementations and their actual licences;
3. proprietary ecosystem implementations;
4. third-party background IP;
5. jointly developed foreground IP;
6. confidential evidence and customer data;
7. trademarks, certification marks and commercial service rights.

Do not assume that a document licence automatically governs software, brands, datasets, patents or services. Verify each repository and artifact separately.

Open components should advance adoption, interoperability, reproducibility or standard credibility. Proprietary components should protect security-sensitive implementation, enterprise operations, advanced optimisation, connectors, policy engines, deployment control or other defensible commercial value.

Before public disclosure, assess patent, trade-secret, confidentiality, export-control, security and partner-consent implications with qualified professionals where material.

## 20. Proposed v0.15 realignment and change control

If accepted, this proposal makes v0.15-experimental-MSC1 the source for new ecosystem operating decisions. It does not migrate existing products or promote v0.15 profiles to implemented status.

For every product, before a material milestone:

1. verify the pinned source commit, blob and digest;
2. review EEC-Q, SF, CFS, RGP, WPC, MSC and REC individually using §7.1;
3. select only task-relevant capabilities for implementation, keeping other dispositions explicit;
4. map current components, semantics, data, IDs, APIs and schemas to the selected capabilities;
5. record limitations, evidence scope, unsupported behavior and migration posture in the product build map;
6. run compatibility checks before any source-pin or public-contract migration;
7. keep product adoption, profile conformance, empirical benefit and calculus completion as distinct decisions.

The v0.12.1/RGP2, v0.13/RWP1 and v0.14/REC1 source files and all dependent registries, proofs, tests, fixtures and APIs retain their exact historical identity. A v0.15 alignment record must not relabel a historical artifact as v0.15 evidence. Existing product pins move only under a separately reviewed migration that states affected consumers, preservation/loss, compatibility results and rollback or parallel-support posture.

For this proposal, the affected consumers are all E7G-T-family products and their build maps, downstream adapters and documentation. The preservation rule is to retain native product meanings and historical pins. The migration change, if accepted, is limited to future source selection and the review-record schema; it does not rewrite product semantics, IDs or APIs. Compatibility is checked by retaining historical source records, validating the new source digest, checking each product's seven-family assessment and rerunning the product-specific compatibility tests named in its migration record.

EEC-Q, SF, CFS, RGP, WPC, MSC and REC remain independently gated. WPC Distributed retains its unsupported status. E7C and the full calculus remain incomplete. Passing workflows, source inclusion, a profile review or an adapter do not establish full conformance, external truth, domain authority, product adoption or improved AI performance.

## 21. Final operating anchor

If the ecosystem becomes too broad or internally inconsistent, return to these questions:

> What consequential problem are we solving?

> What does E7G-T materially change in the solution?

> What evidence would establish or falsify the proposed value?

> Which reusable capability should remain controlled by the E7G-T ecosystem?

> What is the smallest next move that preserves the path to a high-value system?

Proceed from there.
