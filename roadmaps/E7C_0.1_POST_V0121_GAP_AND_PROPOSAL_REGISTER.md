# Post-v0.12.1 gap and proposal register

## 1. Purpose

This register prevents E7C/0.1 from formalising an incomplete snapshot of
v0.12.1. It records gaps identified after v0.12.1, the proposals intended to
address them, their semantic effect, evidence, compatibility risk and proposed
destination.

This file is a **seed inventory**, not a promotion decision and not a claim of
completeness. Neither inclusion nor an existing implementation makes a proposal
canonical, mathematically sound, product-ready or part of E7C.

## 2. Authority and invariants

- v0.12.1/RGP2 remains the published experimental canonical baseline.
- No entry in this register edits, supersedes or silently reinterprets v0.12.1.
- Later drafts and profiles remain at their recorded maturity.
- E7C must evaluate proposals; it must not assume that every later construct is
  a core primitive.
- Product implementations are evidence and regression material, not authority
  over the calculus.
- A proposal may be admitted only at an explicit destination:
  `e7c_core`, `derived_e7c_library`, `profile_adapter`,
  `experimental_profile`, `product_local`, `defer` or `reject`.
- Unknown compatibility stays `unknown`; it must not be rounded up to
  conservative compatibility.

## 3. Required entry fields

Every entry must record:

| Field | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| ID | Stable register identifier |
| Gap | What v0.12.1 cannot currently express, distinguish or establish |
| Proposal | Construction or correction offered |
| Semantic effect | Clarification, correction, conservative extension, new mathematics, conjecture or product mechanism |
| Dependencies | Exact baseline constructs and source pins |
| Compatibility | Conservative, breaking, mixed or unknown, with rationale |
| Evidence | Example, proof, counterexample, bounded model, implementation or empirical result |
| Proposed destination | One destination from the controlled vocabulary |
| Decision status | Discovered, triaged, admitted for testing, validated, rejected or deferred |
| Open obligations | What must be resolved before disposition |

## 5. Missing-source capture

WP0 must actively search for and register:

1. originating-author suggestions made after the v0.12.1 publication;
2. superseded or alternate v0.12.2/v0.13 drafts, review sheets and
   counterexamples, whether or not merged;
3. issues, PR discussions and product discoveries that expose a kernel gap;
4. product-local constructs that may indicate a reusable abstraction;
5. rejected proposals, so they cannot silently return under new names.

For every discovered item, WP0 must pin a commit, file digest, issue/PR URL or
other immutable source reference. If a source cannot be recovered, record the
missing source and its known claim; do not reconstruct it from memory and call
that reconstruction authoritative.

## 6. Admission and closure rules

A proposal is ready for foundation use only when:

1. its gap is demonstrated by a concrete example or counterexample;
2. its semantics and non-claims are stated;
3. its dependencies and exact source pins are recorded;
4. compatibility with v0.12.1 is classified with evidence;
5. affected equality, failure, loss, identity, scope, authority and temporal
   distinctions are identified;
6. its destination is reviewed;
7. required laws, witnesses and counterexamples are named;
8. any bounded evidence is labelled bounded;
9. product evidence is not substituted for mathematical validation.

WP0 cannot close while a known post-v0.12.1 proposal lacks an entry or while a
material entry has no reviewed destination. WP1 may draft neutral vocabulary in
parallel, but no E7C vertical-slice implementation may begin until this register
has a recorded review verdict of either:

- `complete_for_initial_foundation_slice`; or
- `complete_with_named_deferrals`.

## 7. Product-development hold

Until the initial-foundation-slice verdict:

- preserve current tested product states;
- allow maintenance, security repairs and corrections that do not introduce
  new shared semantics;
- do not begin Q-A3, activate new E2CI semantic profiles, extract a shared
  runtime, stabilise E7-IR, or migrate product identities;
- treat product implementations as differential fixtures for later adapters.

Exceptions require an explicit coordinator decision naming the bounded work,
why it cannot wait, and how it avoids pre-empting the foundation.
