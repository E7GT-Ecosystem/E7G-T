A practical calculus for accountable views.

Use E7G-T to slow down reliance and make projection, loss, reconstruction, bridge mode, and admissible use visible.

Place entity

Name the concrete object, claim, system, output, or representation under review.

Declare projection

State what view is being made and which structure it claims to preserve.

Account for loss

Identify what the view compresses, omits, distorts, or cannot carry.

Limit claim strength

Match confidence and wording to the actual support available.

Declare bridge

Make explicit when you move between source, model, proof, policy, measurement, or action.

Choose next move

Decide whether to rely, inspect, reconstruct, verify, escalate, or stop.

Use cases

E7G-T is designed for public, inspectable work where representations can become overtrusted.

Legal/admin summaries

Public caution

E7G-T is not established mathematics, not empirical physics, not a proof system, not a Theory of Everything, and not a replacement for domain expertise.

Evaluate it as an early-stage modelling language, reasoning discipline, projection-loss accounting tool, claim-strength linter, and bridge-control method.

The source is richer than the view. The view preserves. The view loses. Reliance begins when you know the difference.
