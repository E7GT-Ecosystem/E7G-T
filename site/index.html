Public caution

E7G-T is experimental mathematical research. A stipulated definition, a bounded computer check, a mathematical proof and an empirical finding are different forms of evidence.

Its prototypes do not establish universal theorems, physical mechanisms or performance advantages beyond the explicitly tested scope. Independent mathematical review is required for stronger claims.

The source is richer than the view. The view preserves. The view loses. Reliance begins when you know the difference.
