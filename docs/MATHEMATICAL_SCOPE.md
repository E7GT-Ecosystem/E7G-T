# E7G-T — Mathematical Scope and Claim Standards

**Status:** current project presentation and research-governance guidance, not an amendment to the experimental canonical mathematical source.

## Definition

E7G-T (Geometry-Thinking) is an experimental mathematical research framework concerned with bounded relational configurations, structural and temporal extensions, transformations, projections, reconstruction fibres, inquiry-relative equivalence, composition, and typed computation. Optional profiles provide further formal interpretations, each with pinned carriers, operations, identities, laws and proof obligations.

The research may have been inspired by ideas outside mathematics, including metaphysical intuitions. **Those ideas supply no axioms, definitions, proofs, empirical evidence or conformance requirements.** They are not part of the project's mathematical assertions or public research objectives.

## What the work consists of

1. **Formal objects.** Declare a carrier or class, semantic signatures, admissibility, exact identity, contexts and comparison relations.
2. **Typed operations.** Specify domains, codomains, composition, preservation/loss, partial outcomes and bridges between signatures.
3. **Mathematical results.** Clearly label definitions, conditional propositions, proofs, counterexamples and open conjectures.
4. **Executable evidence.** State exact bounded carriers, algorithms, tests, resource limits, source editions and reproducibility instructions; retain negative results.
5. **Applications and validation.** Treat software tools and domain pilots as separately tested interpretations, not proofs of general mathematics or facts about external systems.

## Claim classification

- **Definition:** true by stipulation only within its declared model.
- **Proved proposition:** derived from named hypotheses in an established mathematical substrate or independently checked proof.
- **Finite-model check:** exhaustively or selectively validated within a stated bounded carrier; no automatic generalisation.
- **Conjecture / research direction:** not yet established by proof.
- **Implementation:** an inspectable program, distinct from a proof of all-input fidelity.
- **Empirical claim:** requires an independently defined observation and validation protocol outside the mathematical kernel.

The distinction between a modelled configuration and an externally instantiated system must remain explicit. No physical or general real-world inference follows solely from formal resemblance or a successful bounded evaluator run.

## Terminology discipline

Terms such as **generation**, **choice**, **constitution**, **whole**, **grounding**, **dimension** and **phase** have only their declared technical meanings. Mathematical rank is not a physical dimension; selecting a member of a family is not an enacted event; grounding maps are typed maps, not claims about the origin of existence.

Avoid presenting mathematical research as a philosophy, spiritual programme, explanation of consciousness, universal ontology or established physical theory. Historical prompts or inspirations do not authorise such claims.

## Version and provenance policy

The E7G-T **v0.15-experimental-MSC1** source and its inherited editions remain edition-pinned and unchanged by this presentation update. Previously published experimental and archival content stays available through version history for citation and audit. It is not silently rewritten or assigned new mathematical status.

The separately versioned **GCD/0.2** module is non-canonical mathematical research: its finite checks and deterministic receipts are first-party implementation evidence, with independent review and any promotion decision still outstanding.

Existing ecosystem, product and operating-kernel pins are unchanged. Any new mathematical edition or product adoption requires a separate review, versioned migration record and explicit decision.
